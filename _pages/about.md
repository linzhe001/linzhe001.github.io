I am a researcher in surgical robotics and medical AI, currently a visiting student researcher in the Department of Thoracic Surgery at Tianjin Union Medical Center, working with [Dr. Chunyang Jiang](https://medical.nankai.edu.cn/2025/0514/c6623a570482/page.htm). Watching thoracic surgery up close showed me the problem I want to solve: the lung deforms during an operation, so the preoperative images that guide the surgeon no longer match the patient. My goal is to bring spatial reasoning into the operating room and keep those images aligned with the patient throughout surgery.

My research spans 3D reconstruction and registration, surgical scene understanding, and vision-language models. I completed my MSc in Medical Robotics and Artificial Intelligence at University College London and now work as a research assistant at the University of Manchester, both with [Dr. Mobarak I. Hoque](https://mobarakol.github.io/). At Manchester, I built C3VDReg, a public benchmark for colonoscopic point cloud registration, and work on MRI-video registration for pituitary surgery. My bachelor's degree is in mechanical engineering, and it shapes how I approach research: I care about translating learning-based methods into practice. In the longer term, I hope to build a surgical navigation system that reduces the surgeon's cognitive load, and eventually a semi-autonomous robotic endoscope holder that operates under the surgeon's supervision and eases staffing pressures. I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027 and am always happy to hear from anyone interested in surgical AI, medical robotics, or clinical translation.
